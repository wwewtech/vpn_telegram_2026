## FAQ

### Which protocol is usually most stable?

In 2026 VLESS/Reality and WireGuard perform best, but it depends on region and ISP.

### Why can a bot temporarily not open?

Overload, domain/route changes, local ISP restrictions. Always check several options.

### What to check before paying?

Trial period, refund terms, live support.
